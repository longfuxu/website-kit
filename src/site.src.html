Why bother

Why a site, and how I built mine

Why have your own site

  • People search your name. Committees, collaborators and students look you up. Your site is the result you write.
  • It outlives your job. A university page ends with the appointment; yourname.com stays yours.
  • Your story, your words. Databases list your papers. Only your site says why they matter.

How I built mine

  1. 2021Bought longfuxu.com at Namecheap.
  2. 2025 · v1Forked al-folio, a free academic template, and hosted it free on GitHub Pages.
  3. 2025 · v2Wrote a one-page brief. An AI agent rebuilt the site, and it is hosted free on Vercel.
Honest note

I am not an HTML coder. My part was to pick a template, hand over my CV and materials, and decide the style and structure. The AI did the building. The hard part was deciding what I wanted and getting it online: v2 took about a dozen deploy attempts, so today we take the simplest path.

What you need

  • A laptop with a browser
  • Your CV as a PDF (a public version: no grant numbers or unpublished work)
  • A headshot saved as photo.jpg
  • A free GitHub account (your username becomes your web address, so pick a professional one) and a free Vercel account (sign up with GitHub).
  • An AI coding agent: Claude Code or Codex (paid plan, about $20 a month).

You are the PI. The AI is your postdoc.

  • You set the direction (sites you admire), supply the materials (CV, photo, links) and check every line.
  • The AI writes the HTML and CSS, fixes the layout on phones and walks you through publishing.
  • The skill you need is writing a clear brief. You already do that for your students.

Step 2 · 3 min · What goes on the site

Organise your CV into three reading depths

DepthWhoWhat they must find
10 secondsEveryoneName, position, institution, photo, one sentence on what you study, email and links (Google Scholar, ORCID, GitHub).
1 minuteCommittees, collaborators2–4 research themes, selected papers with your name in bold, dated news.
10 minutesPeople evaluating youFull publication list, CV, talks, teaching and mentoring, code and data.
Leave off

Unpublished data · grant numbers and dollar amounts · home address and personal phone · anything you would not show in a job talk.

Rule of thumb

Fewer than about 15 papers and no blog: one page with sections is enough.

Prompt · the hardest sentence on your site
From my attached CV, write five versions of a one-sentence summary of my research for a smart undergraduate outside my field. Under 25 words each, no jargon, no hype words.

Step 3 · 10 min, mostly waiting

Give an AI agent the template, your CV and photo. It does the rest.

  1. Once: install an agent (Claude Code or Codex) and log in to GitHub and Vercel from the terminal (each opens a link you approve in the browser):
    gh auth login npm i -g vercel@latest && vercel login
  2. Want name.github.io? name must be your GitHub username, or a free organization you create with that name.
  3. Put your CV and photo in one folder, open a terminal there and start the agent: claude
  4. Paste the prompt, replace the [highlighted] parts, press Enter. About ten minutes later you have two live links.
Tested today

Step 4 · 5 min · Free hosting

Publish it: yourusername.github.io and name.vercel.app

Address: yourusername.github.io

GitHub Pages

  1. The agent does these steps for you; this is what happens, and how to do it by hand. A push starts Deploy site in your repository’s Actions tab (about 2–4 minutes). It builds the site into a branch called gh-pages.
  2. Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch → choose gh-pages and / (root) → Save. Change it even if another branch is already selected.
  3. About a minute later: https://yourusername.github.io.

Red ✗ at “Setup Ruby”: set ruby-version: "3.3" in .github/workflows/deploy.yml. Red ✗ with “permission denied”: Settings → Actions → General → Workflow permissions → Read and write. Repository not named yourusername.github.io: set baseurl: /repository-name in _config.yml; the site is at yourusername.github.io/repository-name.

Address: name.vercel.app · what I use

Vercel

  1. Log in once (free Hobby plan): npm i -g vercel@latest && vercel login
  2. Take the site GitHub already built (the gh-pages branch) and deploy it as plain files. Replace every yourname:
Terminal · deploy the built site to Vercel
git clone -b gh-pages --depth 1 https://github.com/yourname/yourname.github.io.git yourname
cd yourname && rm -rf .git requirements.txt && echo '{"framework": null}' > vercel.json
vercel deploy --prod --yes

The folder name becomes the project name, so the address is https://yourname.vercel.app if nobody has taken it; otherwise Vercel adds a suffix, which you can change under Settings → Domains. Run the same three lines again after each update.

Pause point

Your site is live on both. The domain can wait for another day.

Wait minutes to hours, then tick Enforce HTTPS. Values from GitHub’s guide.

al-folio only: also set url: https://yourname.com and an empty baseurl in _config.yml, and add a file named CNAME containing yourname.com to the repository, so every rebuild keeps the domain.

Connect it to Vercel

Project Settings → Domains → Add → yourname.com, then copy the records Vercel shows into your registrar. Or point the domain’s nameservers to Vercel, which is my setup (Namecheap → Vercel).

To take home

Four things to remember

Buy yourname.com now

About $11 a year, and it stays yours for your whole career.

Free hosting is enough

GitHub Pages and Vercel serve an academic site for $0.

You do not need to code

You need a clear brief and good materials.

Update it monthly

One news line, any new paper. Old news looks worse than none. Add the site to Google Search Console so a search for your name finds it.

Troubleshooting
ProblemFix
“Deploy site” fails at Setup RubyIn .github/workflows/deploy.yml set ruby-version: "3.3" and push again.
“Deploy site” fails with permission deniedSettings → Actions → General → Workflow permissions → Read and write.
404 at yourname.github.ioSettings → Pages must say gh-pages / (root). The repository must be named exactly yourname.github.io and owned by the account or organization yourname. Wait a minute and reload.
Want a name that is not your usernameCreate a free GitHub organization with that name; you manage it from your own account.
Your name shows as “Name R. Xu”Clear middle_name: in _config.yml.
Vercel: “No python entrypoint found”Delete requirements.txt from the folder you deploy and add vercel.json with {"framework": null}.
The address is name-xyz.vercel.appSomeone already has name.vercel.app. Pick another name under Settings → Domains.
The AI added a paper or award I do not haveDelete it and remind it: “Use only facts from my CV.”
Custom domain not working yetDNS can take hours. Check the four A records and the www CNAME, remove parking records, then enable HTTPS.
